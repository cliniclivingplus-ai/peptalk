There are quite a few compounds that we talk about, SS-31, MOTS-C, NAD+, etc that do something with the mitochondria. And every time you hear somebody try to explain what these compounds do, they're going to use terms like ATP production, electron transport chain, reactive oxygen species, and those terms are constantly getting dropped. Most people just end up nodding along and really don't understand what they're even talking about. 

The purpose of this course is to fix that problem. By the time you finish this, you will have an understanding of:

what the mitochondria actually does

why it matters for how you feel and how you age

what goes wrong with it over time

exactly what these compounds are going to do and why
