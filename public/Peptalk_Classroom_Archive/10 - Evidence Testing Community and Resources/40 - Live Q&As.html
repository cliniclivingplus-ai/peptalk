# 07-30 Education Class: Peptides, Dosing, and Health Supplements

## Key Learnings

1. Stacking Peptides with HRT and Managing Energy Levels: A discussion on which peptides to continue or pause when starting Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT), with a focus on accurately assessing the therapy's effectiveness on energy levels.

2. General Dosing Strategy: Start Low, Go Slow: A recommended principle for starting any new peptide or supplement, particularly those requiring titration. The strategy is to begin with a low dose and increase it gradually to manage side effects and determine the minimum effective dose.

3. Interpreting Clinical Data: A Retatrutide Case Study: An analysis of a Retatrutide clinical trial graph to illustrate how to evaluate data on dosage, efficacy, and side effects. The key takeaway is the concept of diminishing returns, where higher doses provide minimal additional benefits while significantly increasing side effects and cost.

4. The Nature of Clinical Trials and AI Interpretation: An explanation of how clinical trials are designed to reflect real-world scenarios and how AI can be used as a tool to interpret their complex data, along with a warning about its limitations.

5. Rapid Fire Q&A on Various Compounds: A series of questions from the audience and answers regarding specific compounds, their use, and potential concerns.

6. Maximizing AOD 9604 Efficacy with L-Carnitine and Fasted Cardio: AOD 9604 functions by breaking down triglycerides into free fatty acids, releasing them from fat cells. It is not a stimulant-based fat burner. Its effectiveness can be enhanced through complementary actions like pairing it with L-Carnitine or performing fasted cardio.

7. Using AOD 9604 to Overcome Weight Loss Plateaus: AOD 9604 is presented as a cost-effective tool for breaking through weight loss stalls, with its effectiveness demonstrated through a user's case study.

8. AOD 9604 Dosing and Safety: A discussion on the typical and maximum effective dosage for AOD 9604, its safety profile, and how to assess the impact of a dosage increase.

9. L-Carnitine: Oral vs. Injectable Forms: A comparison of the bioavailability and effectiveness of oral versus injectable L-Carnitine.

10. Overcoming Injection Hesitancy: An explanation and visual comparison to address the fear associated with injections, focusing on needle sizes.

11. Peptides and Anti-Doping in Sports (WADA/USADA): Guidance on navigating the use of performance-enhancing peptides for competitive athletes, focusing on anti-doping regulations and detection times.

12. Tesamorelin Usage and Effects: A discussion on the dosage of Tesamorelin, its potential side effects like water retention, and its effectiveness based on user experiences. It covers how dosage and hydration levels can influence outcomes.

13. NAD+ and 5-Amino Blend Dosing: Guidance on appropriate dosages for NAD+ and a 5-amino NADH blend, comparing a user's current regimen to higher clinical and user-administered doses to determine if it's excessive.

14. The Importance and Frequency of Blood Work: A detailed explanation on the necessity of regular blood work to monitor health, objectively track the effectiveness of treatments like peptides, and establish a baseline for health markers.

## Explanations

### 1. Stacking Peptides with HRT and Managing Energy Levels

- Key Points

- A common advanced stack includes HRT, growth hormone secretagogues, and GLP-1 agonists, referred to as the 'mega trio' for overall benefits.

- When starting HRT, it is advisable to discontinue peptides taken specifically for energy, such as MOTC.

- This allows for a clear assessment of whether HRT itself is resolving fatigue issues, without confounding variables.

- It can take four to six months to experience the full benefits of hormone replacement therapy.

- The body eventually adapts to HRT, and it becomes the 'new normal'. If fatigue returns after a prolonged period (e.g., a couple of years), energy-boosting peptides like NAD can be reintroduced.

- Peptides used for non-energy-related benefits, such as Lipo-C for fat burning or cardiovascular health, can be continued.

- Explanation

- Ralph's Question on his CRT Stack

> Ralph, who is starting on CRT (assumed HRT), asked if he should discontinue his current stack.

1. [Hack] advised that to properly assess how hormone therapy impacts his fatigue, Ralph should consider stopping energy-focused supplements like MOTC. This helps determine if the HRT is working as intended for energy improvement. After 4-6 months, or if fatigue comes back, he could then reintroduce something like NAD which he knows works for him for energy. The goal is to isolate the effects of the HRT.

### 2. General Dosing Strategy: Start Low, Go Slow

- Key Points

- It is always safer to start with a lower dose and increase it over time.

- The worst-case scenario of starting too low is a 'wasted' week at a dose that wasn't fully effective.

- Starting with too high a dose can lead to significant side effects (e.g., nausea, increased heart rate with GLPs), which must resolve before the dosage can be adjusted.

- This method helps in understanding individual tolerance and response.

- Starting low is also more cost-effective as it prevents using more of a compound than necessary.

- Explanation

- Dosing 5-Amino-1-MQ with NADH Blend

> A user inquired about the correct dosage for a 5-amino with NADH blend. [Hack] noted that while most amino blends are formulated for 50-100 units, it's wise to start the NADH blend at a lower dose, such as 20 or 25 units. Another user, Jason, shared his experience of taking 30 units of the blend daily along with 10mg of NAD+.

1. The speaker recommends a starting dose of 25 units for the 5-amino NADH blend. If this dose is well-tolerated but not effective enough, it can be increased towards 50 or 75 units. This illustrates the 'start low, go slow' principle to find a personalized, effective dose while minimizing potential side effects.

### 3. Interpreting Clinical Data: A Retatrutide Case Study

- Key Points

- When analyzing clinical trial graphs, understand the data labels (e.g., 'n' for number of participants, '%' for the percentage of the group).

- Higher doses do not always yield proportionally better outcomes.

- Side effects, especially nausea, tend to increase substantially with higher initial doses.

- Evaluate cost-effectiveness: a significant increase in dose and cost for a minor improvement may not be a logical choice.

- Most weight loss in such trials occurs in the initial weeks, even at lower doses.

- Explanation

- Case Study: Retatrutide Dosage vs. Efficacy and Side Effects

> The speaker presented a Retatrutide clinical trial graph. The data showed that increasing the dose from 8mg to 12mg resulted in a maximum of 2.5% additional body weight loss over a year. For a 200-pound individual, this equates to only 5 extra pounds.

1. The speaker explained that this small gain comes with a 50% increase in the amount of the compound used and a much higher incidence of side effects like nausea. For instance, starting at 2mg and titrating to 8mg offered a better risk-reward profile than starting at 4mg and going to 12mg. The conclusion is that for a marginal extra weight loss of about 0.1 pounds per week, the increased cost and negative side effects of the highest dose are not justified for most users.

### 4. The Nature of Clinical Trials and AI Interpretation

- Key Points

- Pharmaceutical companies conduct trials on diverse populations living their normal lives to gauge a drug's effectiveness in realistic conditions, not in a perfectly controlled lab setting.

- The Netflix documentary 'You Are What You Eat: A Twin Experiment' is recommended for insight into how clinical trials are conducted.

- AI tools like ChatGPT can be used to analyze and summarize complex data from charts and graphs.

- To use AI, you can paste data or upload an image and ask for a summary in simple terms.

- Caution is advised when using AI for information retrieval on niche topics, as it can be inaccurate. It is crucial to verify the information from other sources.

- Explanation

- Example of AI Error: Bioglutide

> The speaker shared that multiple people reported that ChatGPT incorrectly identified bioglutide as semaglutide.

1. This serves as a cautionary tale. A simple Google search would have provided the correct information. While AI is useful for analyzing provided data, it can be unreliable for factual recall on less common subjects, highlighting the need for verification.

### 5. Rapid Fire Q&A on Various Compounds

- Key Points

- VIP: After a cycle of 5 units daily for 55 days, a 2 to 4-week break is recommended, though a shorter break is possible.

- Methylene Blue: The speaker personally does not experience significant effects from it and notes that it commonly tastes like dirt.

- Fox Reconstitution: For a vial, using 1.4 mL of bacteriostatic water is recommended, which makes every 10 units on an insulin syringe equivalent to 1 milligram.

### 6. Maximizing AOD 9604 Efficacy with L-Carnitine and Fasted Cardio

- Key Points

- AOD 9604 breaks down triglycerides into free fatty acids.

- Released fatty acids must be burned off to prevent redepositing.

- L-Carnitine helps shuttle these fatty acids to the mitochondria to be used as fuel.

- Fasted cardio (e.g., 30-minute walk) is another effective way to burn the released fat.

- Pairing AOD 9604 with L-Carnitine is complementary, not mandatory.

- Explanation

AOD 9604 facilitates the breakdown of triglycerides and releases free fatty acids into the body. If these fatty acids are not utilized for energy, they can be redeposited back into fat cells. To maximize the fat loss effect, it's beneficial to engage in activities that burn these newly available fatty acids. One method is to supplement with L-Carnitine, which acts as a shuttle, transporting the free fatty acids to the mitochondria to be burned as fuel. An alternative method is to perform fasted cardio, such as walking on a treadmill for 30 minutes in the morning. In a fasted state, the body is primed to use fat for fuel instead of recently consumed carbohydrates. It's a common misconception that L-Carnitine is required for AOD 9604 to work; it is an optional, complementary supplement, not a necessity.

- The 'Telephone Game' Effect of a TikTok Video

> Four months ago, [Hack] made a TikTok video about pairing L-Carnitine with AOD. Subsequently, a widespread belief emerged that one must take L-Carnitine with AOD.

1. This example illustrates how information can propagate and evolve, leading to a common misconception. The original point was that the two compounds can work well together, not that one is essential for the other's function.

### 7. Using AOD 9604 to Overcome Weight Loss Plateaus

- Key Points

- AOD 9604 can be effective for breaking weight loss plateaus.

- It is a cost-effective option compared to other compounds.

- A 5mg vial can last for 16 days and costs around $39.

- If it's the only variable changed, results can be attributed to the compound.

- Users may also experience a reduction in inches.

- It can be used strategically just to break a stall and then discontinued.

- Explanation

When an individual experiences a prolonged weight loss stall (e.g., three months), introducing AOD 9604 can be an effective strategy. If adding the compound is the only variable changed in an otherwise consistent routine, any subsequent weight loss can likely be attributed to it. It is highlighted as an inexpensive option, with a speaker mentioning a price of $39 for a 16-day supply. Once the stall is broken, for instance, by losing a kilogram, one could discontinue its use. Some users also report non-scale victories, such as losing inches and their pants feeling looser.

- Case Study: Michael Breaking a Weight Loss Stall

> [Speaker 5], Michael, reported being stuck at a weight of 245 lbs after dropping from 288 lbs. His routine consisted of cardio on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, and lifting on Monday, Wednesday, Friday. He introduced AOD pre-workout. This was the only change he made. He successfully broke his plateau and his weight dropped to 241 lbs.

1. This case study demonstrates the potential of AOD 9604 to break a weight loss plateau. With all other factors like diet and exercise remaining constant, the introduction of AOD was the catalyst that led to a resumption of weight loss, supporting the claim of its effectiveness for this purpose.

### 8. AOD 9604 Dosing and Safety

- Key Points

- A common and effective dose is 300 micrograms.

- The dose can be increased to as high as 500 micrograms.

- To evaluate a higher dose, assess results over a couple of weeks, not just one.

- AOD 9604 has a high safety profile with minimal side effects.

- Explanation

A standard and often effective dose for AOD 9604 is 300 micrograms. While users can increase the dosage up to 500 micrograms, the 300 mcg dose is typically sufficient to produce results, such as breaking a stall. If one chooses to increase the dose to 500 mcg, it is advised to assess its effectiveness over a period of a couple of weeks to determine if it leads to a greater rate of weight loss compared to the 300 mcg dose. A single week's result is not enough to draw a conclusion. AOD 9604 is noted to have a good safety profile with virtually no negative side effects, making experimentation with higher doses relatively low-risk.

- User Inquiry on Increasing AOD Dosage

> [Speaker 6], who is currently using AOD at a dose of 250 or 300 mcg, inquired about increasing it to 500 mcg to see if it would yield a better effect as they conclude their current cycle.

1. [Hack] advised that while trying the 500 mcg dose is safe and permissible, the 300 mcg dose is generally effective. He recommended that if [Speaker 6] tries the higher dose, they should monitor their rate of weight loss over several weeks to make a valid comparison against the results from the 300 mcg dose.

### 9. L-Carnitine: Oral vs. Injectable Forms

- Key Points

- Injectable L-Carnitine is more bioavailable and effective.

- Oral L-Carnitine is a viable alternative for those who dislike injections.

- Avoid taking excessively high doses of oral L-Carnitine.

- Explanation

Injectable L-Carnitine is more bioavailable and effective than its oral counterpart. However, for individuals who are averse to injections or experience soreness from them, the oral form can be a sufficient alternative. It is important not to consume excessively high amounts of oral L-Carnitine.

### 10. Overcoming Injection Hesitancy

- Key Points

- The length difference between a 5/16th inch and a half-inch 30 gauge needle is minimal.

- The sensation between the two sizes is not significantly different.

- A 30 gauge, half-inch needle is a good starting point for those with injection anxiety.

- Explanation

To alleviate concerns about injections, a comparison was made between a 5/16th inch needle and a half-inch needle, both of a 30 gauge. The actual difference in length is minimal and the sensation is not significantly different. The visual perception of the size difference is often greater than the physical reality. For those hesitant about injections, starting with a 30 gauge, half-inch needle is suggested as a comfortable option.

### 11. Peptides and Anti-Doping in Sports (WADA/USADA)

- Key Points

- Athletes must check the banned substance list of their governing body (e.g., USADA, WADA).

- Understanding the half-life and detection window of a peptide is crucial.

- BPC 157 has a short half-life and may be undetectable in the system after about six hours.

- The first step is to identify the testing organization and find their banned list.

- Explanation

Athletes considering the use of peptides must first identify their sport's governing anti-doping agency, such as WADA (World Anti-Doping Agency) or USADA (United States Anti-Doping Agency). The next step is to consult the agency's official banned substance list. The detection window for substances is also a critical factor. For example, BPC 157 was noted to be undetectable in animal systems approximately six hours after administration, suggesting that with strategic timing, it might not be detected in a test. The primary advice is to first find out the testing federation and then research their specific banned list.

- Athlete's Inquiry about Banned Substances

> [Speaker 6], who is considering a return to competitive sports, asked which peptides are on the banned substance list for organizations like USADA.

1. The advice given was to first confirm the testing body, which was identified as USADA. Then, the athlete should search for the official USADA banned substance list. The example of BPC 157's short detection time was used to illustrate that understanding the properties of each compound is as important as knowing if it's on the banned list.

### 12. Tesamorelin Usage and Effects

- Key Points

- Dosage can influence side effects; lower doses (e.g., half a milligram) may result in less water retention than higher doses.

- Adequate water intake, such as a gallon and a half a day with electrolytes, can help mitigate water retention by flushing the system.

- The effectiveness of Tesamorelin is not negated by the absence of initial weight gain; significant results can be observed over the course of a cycle.

- Dietary habits, such as not following a low-carb or keto diet, are also a variable in the overall results.

- Explanation

- Case Study: Tesamorelin Dosage, Hydration, and Results

> [Speaker 8] reports being down 45 pounds and losing about two a week while using half a milligram of Tesamorelin. They drink about a gallon and a half of water daily plus electrolytes. [Hack] suggests this low dosage and high water intake could be why they haven't experienced significant water retention.

1. [Hack] explains that a higher dosage might have led to more water retention, and [Speaker 8]'s high water intake is effectively flushing their system.

2. [Speaker 9], who is three-quarters of the way through a cycle, corroborates that the treatment is working, having seen 'significant changes' without experiencing weight gain at the beginning. This shows that initial weight gain isn't a universal indicator of the peptide's efficacy.

### 13. NAD+ and 5-Amino Blend Dosing

- Key Points

- A regimen of 75mg of NAD+ three times a week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), supplemented with 25 units of a 5-amino NADH blend on other days (Tuesday, Thursday), is considered a safe and effective dosage.

- For context, NAD IV infusions can be as high as 600mg, and some individuals inject 200mg of NAD+ three times a week.

- An excessive dosage might involve running 100 units of the 5-amino NADH blend and 200mg of NAD+ six days a week, which would likely necessitate a break.

- Explanation

- Example Dosing Regimen for NAD+ and 5-Amino Blend

> [Speaker 8] describes their protocol: taking 75mg of NAD+ on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, and on Tuesday and Thursday, adding 25 units of a 5-amino NADH blend via IM injection. They ask if this is too much.

1. [Hack] confirms this dosage is 'fine' and not excessive. They provide context by comparing it to much higher doses, such as 600mg IV infusions or other users running 200mg three times a week, concluding that [Speaker 8]'s current protocol is 'pretty solid'.

### 14. The Importance and Frequency of Blood Work

- Key Points

- Blood work is crucial to objectively measure if treatments and supplements are working, as subjective feelings can be misleading.

- It allows for tracking specific markers to assess efficacy, such as inflammatory markers for BPC-157 or heart markers like HRV for Cardiogen.

- A baseline lab panel before starting a new protocol is recommended to track changes over time.

- The ideal frequency for blood work is whenever a protocol is changed. A more practical schedule is every six months (preferred) or at least once a year.

- Options for getting blood work include through a doctor or paying out-of-pocket, with some services like AlgoRx offering basic panels for around $200.

- Explanation

- Case Study: Measurable Health Improvements

> [Speaker 8], aged 53, reports that their blood pressure has consistently been excellent, not exceeding 128 over 78. Their doctor remarked that this is the blood pressure of a 30 to 35-year-old.

1. [Hack] highlights this as a perfect example of a positive, measurable outcome that can be tracked. This serves as a lead-in to the broader discussion on why getting blood work is important to see tangible proof that a health regimen is effective.

- Tracking Peptide Efficacy (e.g., Cardiogen)

> How can one know if a peptide like Cardiogen is genuinely improving heart function? [Hack] points out that without looking at specific metrics like HRV or other heart-related markers in blood work, it's impossible to know for sure, as one might not 'feel' the benefits directly.

1. This example illustrates the core argument for getting blood work done. Many benefits, especially at the organ or cellular level, are not subjectively felt. Blood work provides the objective data needed to confirm if a treatment is having the desired physiological effect.
