Overview

Hormone replacement therapy has a lot of controversy surrounding it and a lot of times it is really misunderstood. People look at things from a perspective that is either based on outdated information or just a lack of knowledge and understanding. The goal here is to cover both sides of this spectrum and look at how people treat diagnosed deficiencies and diseases as well as how to optimize to bring things back in terms of quality of life.

This course is going to be pretty big and complex compared to some of the other courses because we're breaking down all of the things that are involved, as well as the differences between men and women, and some of the overlapping and underlying principles that may be associated with both of them.

Nothing in this course is meant to be a protocol but rather an understanding of how these systems look and work. The best thing that you can do is work with a medical provider to have hormone replacement therapy prescribed to you. I work with M&M Health and that is who I would highly recommend to work with if you want to pursue hormone replacement therapy.
