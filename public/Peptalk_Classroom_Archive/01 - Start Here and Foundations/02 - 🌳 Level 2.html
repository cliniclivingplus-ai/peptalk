🏃‍♂️ How to Best Use This

Most people don't struggle because they lack information.

They struggle because they don't know where to look, what to ignore, or how to decide.

1️⃣ Start With Your Goal

Before asking what to take, be clear on what you're trying to change:

  • Fat loss / metabolic health

  • Recovery or injury support

  • Labs and biomarkers

  • Cognitive performance

  • Longevity / aging

Once the goal is clear, everything becomes easier.

2️⃣ Use Search When Looking for Answers

Chances are, you might find a discussion thread for this.

Search helps you:

  • See multiple perspectives

  • Spot patterns

..and if you don't find what you need, make a post!

3️⃣ How to Ask Questions That Get Good Answers

The best questions include:

  • your goal

  • what you've already tried or researched

  • any relevant context

Vague questions get vague answers.

Clear questions get useful ones.

🟢 Step 2:

You don't need to complete steps, earn points, or post on a schedule.

Use Hacksmith's Peptalk when:

  • something doesn't make sense

  • you see conflicting information

  • you're looking for perspectives

  • you're deciding whether to do something at all.

Reading is fine. Asking is fine. Taking your time is expected.

What to do next (optional, not a task)

If you want direction:

  • browse recent discussions

  • explore cheat sheets

  • read a deep dive that matches your goals

Good decisions don't come from urgency.

They come from understanding.
