Overview

P21, also referred to as P021, is a synthetic peptide that was designed to support neuron growth and protection in the brain. Most of the research behind it has focused on Alzheimer's disease and age-related cognitive decline but it is also typically used as a general cognitive enhancer and a neuroprotective compound by people who don't have Alzheimer's.

Mechanism of Action

Neurogenesis: One thing that your brain is going to do throughout your life is grow new neurons. Most of the time it occurs in the hippocampus, which is the part of your brain that is most involved in memory formation and learning.

Your body regulates the rate of new neuron production and one of the signals that your body uses to suppress it is a molecule called LIF (Leukemia Inhibitory Factor). LIF activates this signaling pathway called JAK-STAT3, which basically tells your brain's neural stem cells to slow down, so it's like an engine governor on your brain's neurons. P21 blocks that suppression signal and showed that it could reduce the STAT3 activation by about 30% in animals.

Basically what P21 is doing is taking off the governor and letting your brain proliferate (dividing) and differentiate (transforming) into new neurons at a higher rate. So instead of increasing neurogenesis, it's basically removing the restraint that prevents it, which ultimately results in a better safety profile.

BDNF Increases: This component of P21 is where it really gets interesting when it comes to Alzheimer's and neuroprotection. P21 increases the production of BDNF, or brain-derived neurotrophic factor, which is basically a growth and maintenance signal for neurons in the brain. It helps with neuron survival, promotes the formation of new synaptic connections, and plays a very important role in learning and memory. Low BDNF is associated with things like depression, cognitive decline, and Alzheimer's. Higher BDNF is one of the main things that you are trying to optimize when you are looking at overall brain health.

When BDNF increases, it creates a reaction inside of your neurons, which turns down this enzyme called GSK-3beta. Here's why that is important. Your neurons have a support structure inside of them that contains a protein called tau that helps hold it together and keep everything running how it should. When things go wrong like aging or disease, this GSK enzyme becomes overactive and starts to attack that structure, which puts too many signals onto tau and causes it to clump and tangle up instead of doing what it's supposed to. Those clumps are one of the major things that go wrong when it comes to Alzheimer's disease. When the neurons are full of tangles, they will stop functioning and eventually die off.

P21 through BDNF basically puts the brakes on that enzyme. Less activity means that you will have less damage to that structural support system, which means that you will ultimately have less tangle formation and your neurons will be healthier. The animal study showed about a 50% reduction in this type of damage in the treated animals, which is a pretty large number. It also was able to reduce amyloid beta, which is the other major component of Alzheimer's, but that was less consistent than the tau improvement.

So all of this basically means that you will have:

  • new neuronal growth

  • less tangle build-up in the brain

  • stronger synaptic connections

  • better neuroprotection and cell survival

Benefits of P-21

Cognitive Enhancement and Memory: The most consistently documented benefit is improved learning and memory performance and they were able to observe that in both diseased and healthy animals so it's not just fixing things when you're sick. They were able to look at normal healthy mice and see a significant improvement in object recognition and spatial memory after treatment with P-21. In aged rats after 88 days, they saw improved spatial memory but it wasn't brought back to the level it was for the young animals. It also shows how it may be able to bring things back, but not perfectly, within humans as they age.

In Alzheimer's models that started treatment early, cognitive performance was basically maintained at normal levels over the course of nine to 15 months. That's a pretty long time especially to see consistency of the effects through different methods of testing. That's really the most promising component of the preclinical data.

Now over the course of a few weeks, what people are noticing is that they start to have:

  • clearer thinking

  • better word retrieval

  • improved recall

  • better focus under cognitive load

The mechanism really explains why it happens over that timeline. You were growing new neurons and waiting for them to basically mature and integrate into the functional circuits in your brain so it's not going to be an overnight process.

Neuroprotection and Brain Aging: The tau damage reduction is one of the most significant components of the preclinical data and those problems aren't exclusive just to Alzheimer's. The same processes that will turn into that neurodegeneration and disease are also happening in a healthy aging brain but at a slower rate. By targeting this, P-21 is addressing something that is relevant way before somebody is even concerned about Alzheimer's.

Synapse loss is also a strong predictor of cognitive decline compared to things like amyloid plaques or tau tangles in a lot of analyses. When P-21 helps to preserve and enhance the synaptic proteins, it is basically protecting the molecular components of cognition most directly connected to real-world cognitive function. For someone who is not dealing with any neurodegeneration but wants to preserve the sharp functions as they age (which is most people), the things that P-21 is targeting are where you really want to focus.

Neurogenesis: The hippocampus will continue to generate new neurons throughout your adult life and those neurons are important for memory and learning capacity. P-21 created about a 4x increase in hippocampal neurogenesis in animal studies that were in diseased animals with severely suppressed baseline neurogenesis. For healthy users the effect would likely be smaller but the same mechanism can still apply. Taking that governor off of the LIF/STAT3 pathway and increasing BDNF will push neurogenesis above what the brain would otherwise be producing.

Retinal Health: This one isn't as common for P-21 but is an extra benefit that is worth knowing about. They looked at aged rats and Alzheimer's model mice and saw that P-21 prevented age-related macular degeneration like pathology, by reducing photoreceptor degeneration, retinal tau accumulation, amyloid beta, and abnormal vessel growth. The retina is basically like brain tissue that sits within your eye and is highly energy demanding and has the similar vulnerability to tau and amyloid pathology like the rest of the brain. While it hasn't been seen in humans, it is something that you may notice as a benefit.

Dosage and Protocols

P-21 is typically used either subcutaneously or as an intranasal spray. My preferred way of using it is intranasal, which is similar for all of the cognitive compounds. As you are able to leverage the olfactory and trigeminal nerve pathways that run through the nasal cavity.

The common dosage ranges are below:

  • Intranasal: The lower end would be 500mcg - 1mg daily. Some will go higher, like 1 - 2mg daily. I prefer using 1mg personally.

  • Subcutaneous: The same dosage ranges apply for subcutaneous administration.

  • Cycle length: Minimum of 4 weeks, but ideally 8 - 12 weeks is more optimal as we are focusing on sustained neurogenesis over time.

  • Frequency: Daily is preferred, but 5 on 2 off could also work.

Side Effects and Safety

The biggest thing with P-21 is that there are no human studies that we can reference for the safety profile but with the animal studies things seem to be relatively safe overall. From what I have seen there has been nothing that is a major concern but if we had to assume the biggest risk will be something that we just don't know of.
