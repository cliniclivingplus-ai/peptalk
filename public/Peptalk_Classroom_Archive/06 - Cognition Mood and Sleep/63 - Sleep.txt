The goal of this course is to give an understanding of sleep - what's going on in the body, what factors into quality sleep, and how we can improve sleep. Sleep is complex and is one of the most important things we can optimize when it comes to health. The goal here is to look at the actual science of sleep, how it works, sleep habits, supplements, and potential peptide therapy solutions to help promote a better quality of sleep.

If anything, this sleep course probably taught you that there are a LOT of things that factor into sleep. There's the components of the body, stages of sleep, daily habits, nutrition, exercise, stress, supplements, peptides, tech, and the list goes on. Good sleep is going to be multifaceted and there is no singular magic pill that will fix all of the issues surrounding sleep. 

This might all feel overwhelming, but small changes can have pretty significant improvements. Start by identifying one thing and work on that and then you can move on to others. Over time, you may begin to layer in more sleep interventions as needed so you don't need to go all in from the start and throw everything at it. 

The most important thing to remember is that this will be tailored and personalized to you. If you don't identify what your specific sleep issue is a result of, then you may never find the right solution. The most cost effective things to start with are your habits and environment so if those aren't fixed, start there. If you don't fix those, you may never truly fix the issues that you have.
