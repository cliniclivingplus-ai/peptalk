Overview

One of the most common things that people encounter on a weight loss journey is a stall. They are also one of the most frustrating experiences that you can have during that journey. It doesn't matter if you're using a GLP or not, the second the scale stops moving it seems like the entire process has failed.

The purpose of this course is to help you understand what causes the stalls at a biological level and the strategies to address it in a way that makes sense. Every piece of this course exists because it intends to address a specific mechanism that contributes to stalls. I want to explain to you the why before I tell you what to do because it will ultimately allow you to be more consistent with your approach and apply it in the smartest way possible.
