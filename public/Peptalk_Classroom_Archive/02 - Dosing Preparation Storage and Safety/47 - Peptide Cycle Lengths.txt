What is Peptide Cycling?

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/f5524e6d?md=c4308f9bf1714ebc8f700131a701af39

Text saved

A peptide cycle is talking about the length of time that you are using a peptide (or multiple peptides) followed by a scheduled break from those compounds. 

Why Cycling Matters: 

Prevent Diminishing Returns - In some cases, different peptides may lose their effectiveness overtime due to receptor downregulation. By cycling, you can keep those fresh and mitigate this issue. 

Safety - Cycling can help mitigate the risk of long-term side effects depending on the compound. Things like blood pressure issues, insulin resistance, or immune suppression. 

System Reset - This can be viewed similarly to the diminishing returns, but sometimes you just have to give your body a break. Often times, you will feel like you have better results again once you start back. 

Cost Effectiveness - In my opinion, this is an important one. Often times, in an off-cycle, you can keep or retain a lot of the benefits you had before. Why not save a few bucks during that time? 

Cycle Terms: 

On-Cycle: The time that you are using a peptide (ranges from days to months)

Off-Cycle: The time that you are taking a break from using a peptide. Many will be within the 2-8 week range. 

Tapering: This doesn't have to happen, but it can refer to gradually lowering the dose when you're coming off instead of quitting cold turkey.
