Some peptides just… hurt more than others.

Sometimes it’s during injection.
Sometimes it’s hours later.

Here’s what to expect.

🔥 Most Common “Spicy” Injections
🧬 GHK-Cu / GLOW / KLOW

Usually not painful immediately.

A few hours later:
• Feels like a charlie horse
• Deep muscle soreness
• Possible welt

What helps:
• Massage gun after injection
• Go deeper (½” needle)
• Inject in glute instead of stomach
• Add extra BAC water to dilute

🧪 Tesamorelin

• Mild burn during injection
• More noticeable early in cycle

Usually tolerable.

🧊 AOD-9604

• May sting slightly
• Worse if reconstituted with acetic acid

Not severe — just noticeable.

💪 Amino Blends

These are commonly sore.

Why?
• Thicker solutions
• Higher concentrations
• Often larger injection volume

Can:
• Leave welts
• Feel tender for a day or two

Anything containing L-Carnitine falls in this category.

🥩 L-Carnitine

Very common to cause:
• Soreness
• Hard lump
• Temporary welt

Helpful tips:
• Warm the area first (heating pad)
• Inject slowly
• Massage after

⚡ NAD+

• Can burn during injection
• May feel sore afterward

Injection speed matters.

🧄 Glutathione

• Can sting
• May leave site sore

Also:
Yes — it smells like rotten eggs. That’s normal.

🧠 MOTS-C

In my opinion:

The most painful.

• Hurts going in
• Stays sore
• Can itch later
• May cause a mild local reaction

This one just tends to be “spicy.”

🧬 FOXO4-DRI

• Mild burn
• More noticeable later than during injection

Not terrible — just present.

🌙 Epitalon

• Can sting slightly
• Usually mild

🔬 SS-31

• Slight sting
• Mild soreness afterward

Short-lived.

🛠 General Tips to Reduce Injection Pain

• Dilute with more BAC water
• Inject slowly
• Rotate sites
• Use glutes for deeper injections
• Warm the area before
• Massage after
• Stay hydrated

Important Reminder

Pain does NOT mean:
• It’s bad
• It’s contaminated
• It’s harming you

Some compounds are simply more irritating to tissue.

If you notice:
• Severe swelling
• Fever
• Spreading redness
• Signs of infection

That’s different — address that immediately.
