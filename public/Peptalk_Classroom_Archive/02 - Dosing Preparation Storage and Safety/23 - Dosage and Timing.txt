Epitalon

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/007a40c0?md=3b067b73b1374dfdae27287b16485a15

Text saved

Format

Injectable

Common Dosages

Epitalon is difficult. There is a lot of discussion about what the right dosage amount is. There are Russian and Ukranian protocols that recommend one amount, and allegedly were misinterpreted and so it should be a different amount. I will say this - some say the dosage should be somewhere around 100 - 300 mcg. In practical application, most people who use this seem to need to be at a minimum of 1 mg, with some saying they had to get up to the 3, 4, 5mg+ range to get sleep benefits. So, I will lay out what I think the reasonable approach is and highlight alternative protocols below. 

Start as high as you feel is reasonable for the first cycle. If you want to try the 100 - 300 mcg range for 20 days, go for it. If you feel comfortable starting at 1 mg, 2 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, then do that. The length of the cycle is often 10 - 20 days. 

If you get no benefits from it, try another cycle a couple months later at a higher dosage (assuming you didn't start at 10mg). At some point, you will either recognize the dosage amount that gives you the most benefit, or you will realize it might not be for you. 

Timing

Injectable: Typically taken once per day at night before bed. Can be split to morning and night if desired, but in my opinion, just once at night is sufficient. 

Alternative Protocols

1mg daily for 50 days. 

2mg daily for 25 days.
