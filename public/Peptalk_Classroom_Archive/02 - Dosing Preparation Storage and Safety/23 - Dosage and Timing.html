Overview

This course will go over common dosage protocols, timing, and alternative protocols (other ways it can be done that also works).


Disclaimer: This is not medical advice. I am not telling you how much to take. I am not prescribing this to you. Talk to a doctor. But these are the common dosage protocols and ranges that many may typically use.

5-Amino-1MQ

Format

  • Oral or injectable

Common Dosages

  • Oral: 50 - 150 mg daily. Typically, capsules are 50mg/ea.

  • Injectable: 0.5 - 20mg daily. Start low around 0.5 - 1mg to assess tolerance and increase as needed. There is much less usage of injectable 5-Amino, so the protocols can vary quite a bit in what works best.

Timing

  • Oral: Since it can increase energy levels, morning and midday / early afternoon is best. Can be taken with or without food.

  • Injectable: Typically in the morning. Some prefer to take prior to fasted cardio sessions.

Alternative Protocols

  • Can be done 5 days on, 2 days off or even every other day. Both of these protocols can work well and depend on individual preference.

Adamax

Format

  • Nasal spray

Common Dosages

  • Intranasal: 100-200mcg daily is usually sufficient for most. The dosage range CAN go higher, but it is supposed to be more powerful than Semax, so not completely necessary.

Timing

  • This is best used in the morning or before mentally demanding tasks (like work) since it can help with things like focus. Some people may choose to divide their dosages to 2x daily (one morning, one mid-day).

Alternative Protocols

  • This could be done 5 days on, 2 days off, every other day, or as needed. In my opinion, using this as needed is best.

AOD-9604

Format

  • Injectable (preferred) or oral

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: 250 - 500 mcg is usually sufficient for many. My preference is 300 mcg per day (or 5 on, 2 off for cost effectiveness).

Timing

  • For either method, the best time to take it is fasted in the morning, followed by some fasted cardio to help burn fat. Since AOD mobilizes and releases fat, the physical activity, especially in a fasted state, can help to burn it off.

Alternative Protocols

  • This could also be done every other day and dosages can go much higher. AOD basically had no negative side effects observed. In my opinion, AOD will either help you in the 250 - 500 mcg or it won't. There's no real need to do 2mg daily of AOD.

  • Some may split the dose to morning and then afternoon or morning and pre-workout.

  • Some may pair with L-Carnitine. This is NOT NECESSARY, but L-Carnitine helps to shuttle free fatty acids to the mitochondria to be used as fuel, which makes sense as it could burn off the fatty acids that AOD is releasing.

ARA-290

Format

  • Injectable

Common Dosages

  • Protocol can be anywhere from 1 - 4 mg/day. Some even observe benefits at 500 mcg (0.5 mg) daily. The best would be to start low to assess if it works and increase as needed.

Timing

  • Once daily, any time of day.

Alternative Protocols

  • Some may split it up to 2x daily, similar to how BPC-157 may be done. This could be something like 250 mcg (0.25 mg) morning, 250 mcg (0.25mg) evening.

BPC-157

Format

  • Injectable, oral, or intranasal.

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: 250 - 500 mcg daily. Some have administered as high as 5 mg / day with no adverse side effects observed. However, that is cost inefficient.

  • Oral: 500 - 1000 mcg daily.

  • Intranasal: 250 - 500 mcg daily.

Timing

  • Injectable: Once per day is fine, but splitting the dose to half in the morning and half in the evening can be best for continuous inflammation reduction as it has a short half life.

  • Oral: Same as injectable.

  • Intranasal: Same as injectable.

Alternative Protocols

  • Can be done 5 days on, 2 days off or even every other day. In my opinion, doing this one daily is best as it does have a short half life.

Bremelanotide (PT-141)

Format

  • Injectable or intranasal

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: 0.5 - 1.75 mg as needed.

  • Intranasal: 0.5 - 1.75 mg as needed.

Timing

  • Injectable: 30 - 60 minutes before activity.

  • Intranasal: 30 - 60 minutes before activity.

Alternative Protocols

  • Some may take smaller amounts more frequently (like .25mg daily) to keep the libido boosting effects.

  • The difficult thing with PT-141 is you can't really control when the libido hits. It may hit 30 minutes later, it may hit 8 hours later. In my opinion, the best thing to do is start really low (like 0.25 mg) to assess tolerance and response. Then increase over time as needed.

Cagrilintide

Format

  • Injectable

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: The dosage range for Cagrilintide can go from 0.25 mg up to 4.5 mg once weekly. This has a titration schedule similar to GLP-1s. It is best to start VERY LOW (like even 0.125 mg) and move up as needed.

Timing

  • Injectable: Typically injected once per week (or less since it has an 8 day half life). There is no requirement for time of day for this to be taken.

Alternative Protocols

  • Some may take this halfway through the week between GLP-1 dosages to have continued appetite suppression.

  • The one thing I will add is that if you plan to use Cagrilintide, use tdeecalculator.net to ensure you are eating a sufficient amount of calories before introducing this. Many say they're hungry and stalled but are eating 1000 calories per day. They are typically undereating, causing both of those things, which means that Cagrilintide is not the solution, but more of the problem.

DSIP (Delta-Sleep-Inducing Peptide)

Format

  • Injectable or intranasal

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: 100 - 250 mcg (0.1 - 0.25 mg) daily.

  • Intranasal: 100 - 250mcg (0.1 - 0.25 mg) daily.

Timing

  • For both of these methods of administration, they are typically taken 2 - 3 hours before bed.

  • They can also be taken earlier in the day, as many still report the sleep benefits even if taken in the morning.

Alternative Protocols

  • It could also be taken as needed and still yield good results.

Epitalon

Format

  • Injectable

Common Dosages

  • Epitalon is difficult. There is a lot of discussion about what the right dosage amount is. There are Russian and Ukranian protocols that recommend one amount, and allegedly were misinterpreted and so it should be a different amount. I will say this - some say the dosage should be somewhere around 100 - 300 mcg. In practical application, most people who use this seem to need to be at a minimum of 1 mg, with some saying they had to get up to the 3, 4, 5mg+ range to get sleep benefits. So, I will lay out what I think the reasonable approach is and highlight alternative protocols below.

    • Start as high as you feel is reasonable for the first cycle. If you want to try the 100 - 300 mcg range for 20 days, go for it. If you feel comfortable starting at 1 mg, 2 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, then do that. The length of the cycle is often 10 - 20 days.

    • If you get no benefits from it, try another cycle a couple months later at a higher dosage (assuming you didn't start at 10mg). At some point, you will either recognize the dosage amount that gives you the most benefit, or you will realize it might not be for you.

Timing

  • Injectable: Typically taken once per day at night before bed. Can be split to morning and night if desired, but in my opinion, just once at night is sufficient.

Alternative Protocols

  • 1mg daily for 50 days.

  • 2mg daily for 25 days.

FOXO4-DRI

Format

  • Injectable

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: FOXO4-DRI is another one that really has no set dosage ranges. I will list some that are commonly used for this compound.

    • Ben Greenfield does 3mg every other day for 6 days and repeats the cycle 1 - 3x yearly.

    • Others recommend 300 mcg - 1 mg subcutaneously every other day, 1 week on, 1 week off for the total of 1 month.

    • I recently ran 2 mg a day for 7 days and felt like it worked well at that amount.

Timing

  • Injectable: Timing for this compound does not really matter, but I like in the morning.

Alternative Protocols

  • All of the alternate protocols are listed above. One thing to note is that this has no human data. So everything is experimental.

GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

Format

  • Injectable, oral, topical, intranasal

Common Dosages

  • Injectable, oral, intranasal: 1 - 2 mg daily for 4 - 12 weeks on, followed by 4 - 8 weeks off. My preferred protocol is 2 mg daily for 4 - 8 weeks.

  • Topical: Commonly used in serums or creams for skin rejuvenation and hair growth:

    • 1% for facial use,

    • 2% for body application,

    • 3% for promoting hair growth.

Timing

  • Injectable, oral, topical: Once daily. Time of day does not matter.

  • Topical: Apply a topically formulated version after cleansing in the morning and / or night. Allow it to fully absorb before applying other products.

Alternative Protocols

  • Can be done 5 days on, 2 days off or even every other day. Both of these protocols can work fine enough and depend on individual preference.

Glutathione

Format

  • Injectable (IM or SQ), oral, intranasal

Common Dosages

  • Injectable: Typically 100 - 200 mg per injection for a total of 600 - 800 mg/wk. Can be dosed higher depending on the goal. I think the 100 - 200 mg range is sufficient for most.

  • Oral: 250 - 500 mg daily.

  • Intranasal: Glutathione is stinky, but can be fine intranasally. 100 - 200 mg per dose, total of 600 - 800 mg/wk.

Timing

  • Injectable and intranasal: 2 - 3x weekly. Can be used daily if needed. For longer term use, 2 - 3x weekly would be best.

  • Oral: Taken daily.

Alternative Protocols

  • Biggest variance is in frequency and length of cycle. For things like skin lightening, longer term use would be most beneficial as it can take some time.
