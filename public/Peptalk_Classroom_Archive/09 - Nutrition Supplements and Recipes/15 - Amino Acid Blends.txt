5-Amino / NAD+ Blend

Text saved

(NAD+ 100mg/mL / 5-Amino-1MQ 25mg/mL - 30mL)

This is a targeted two-compound blend focused on cellular energy production and fat metabolism at the mitochondrial level. NAD+ and 5-Amino-1MQ work complimentary to each other in a couple of ways - NAD+ fuels the mitochondria directly, while 5-Amino-1MQ inhibits an enzyme that would otherwise suppress fat cell activity and drain NAD+ reserves. Together they create a clean, stimulant-free environment for improved energy, body composition, and cellular function. This blend is popular for anti-aging protocols, metabolic optimization, and anyone looking to enhance fat loss without adding more stimulants or hormone-based compounds.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

NAD+ (100mg/mL): NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell in the body and is central to energy metabolism. It fuels the electron transport chain inside mitochondria, which is how your cells actually produce ATP (usable energy). NAD+ also activates sirtuins which are proteins involved in DNA repair, inflammation regulation, and longevity. Levels decline with age and metabolic stress, and restoring them through injection bypasses the digestive limitations of oral forms. 

5-Amino-1MQ (25mg/mL): 5-Amino-1MQ is a selective NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase) inhibitor. NNMT is an enzyme that, when overactive, depletes NAD+ and keeps fat cells in a low-energy, storage-favoring state. By inhibiting NNMT, 5-Amino-1MQ allows NAD+ levels to rise naturally, activates dormant fat cells, and pushes them toward burning energy rather than storing it. Research has shown it can reduce fat cell size and support favorable changes in body composition without stimulating the central nervous system.

Blend Uses: Best used as part of a metabolic optimization, anti-aging, or body recomposition protocol. Particularly effective when stacked with lifestyle interventions like fasted training, caloric deficits, or time-restricted eating. Also used in longevity-focused protocols where restoring NAD+ levels and improving mitochondrial efficiency are the primary goals.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 20 - 50 units. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week. Some protocols run NAD+ daily or near-daily depending on goals.

Leucine (15mg/mL): Leucine flips on muscle protein synthesis via the mTOR pathway. Even in a fasted or depleted state, this helps trigger recovery and prevent breakdown. Dose is light, but supportive when stacked with other recovery tools.

Isoleucine (7.5mg/mL): Isoleucine helps shuttle glucose into muscle cells and supports endurance during long training sessions. Also assists in rebuilding muscle tissue post-workout.

Valine (7.5mg/mL): Valine acts as a fuel source for muscles during training and helps prevent muscle breakdown. It also supports focus and coordination, especially under fatigue.

Blend Uses: 

Pre-Workout or Intra-Workout: Best used before or during training. Helps improve blood flow, muscular endurance, and recovery between sets. This blend is ideal for anyone doing high-volume lifting, circuit work, or endurance training where fatigue usually sets in fast.

This is a comprehensive injectable B-vitamin and cofactor blend designed to support the building blocks of healthy hair, skin, and nails from the inside out. Each ingredient plays a specific role in cellular energy, keratin production, collagen synthesis, or follicle health. Unlike topical products that work on the surface, this blend delivers nutrients systemically, giving your body what it needs to actually build and repair tissue at the cellular level. It's also a solid general wellness protocol for energy, stress resilience, and metabolic support.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

Niacinamide (50mg/mL): Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) supports NAD+ production, which is central to cellular energy and DNA repair. For skin, it reduces inflammation, improves barrier function, and helps with hyperpigmentation. It also supports keratin production and is one of the most well-researched nutrients for skin health.

Thiamine HCL (50mg/mL): Thiamine (Vitamin B1) is essential for carbohydrate metabolism and nerve function. It plays a foundational role in cellular energy production and helps support the scalp's blood supply and follicle health. Deficiency is linked to hair thinning and poor skin quality.

Pantothenic Acid (25mg/mL): Vitamin B5 is directly involved in the synthesis of Coenzyme A, which is required for fatty acid metabolism and the production of steroid hormones. For hair specifically, it helps strengthen follicles, reduce shedding, and improve thickness. It also supports adrenal health and stress response.

Choline (10mg/mL): Choline supports cell membrane integrity and fat metabolism. Healthy, well-structured cell membranes are fundamental to skin elasticity and hydration. It also supports liver function, which plays a downstream role in hormone balance and skin clarity.

Inositol (10mg/mL): Inositol plays a role in cell signaling and insulin sensitivity. For skin and hair, it helps regulate sebum production and has been studied for its role in reducing hair loss associated with hormonal imbalances, particularly PCOS.

Niacin (5mg/mL): Niacin (a different form of B3 than niacinamide) dilates blood vessels and improves circulation to the scalp and skin. Improved circulation means better nutrient delivery to follicles and surface tissues, supporting both growth and overall skin tone.

Biotin (5mg/mL): Biotin is the most well-known nutrient for hair and nail health. It supports keratin infrastructure which is the structural protein that makes up hair and nails and is frequently linked to improved thickness, growth rate, and reduced brittleness when levels are restored.

Folic Acid (100mcg/mL): Folic acid (Vitamin B9) supports rapid cell division and DNA synthesis — processes that are especially active in hair follicles. It's important for hair growth cycling and helps prevent premature hair loss related to nutritional deficiency.

Riboflavin (100mcg/mL): Riboflavin (Vitamin B2) is a cofactor in the electron transport chain and plays a role in maintaining collagen and mucous membranes. It supports antioxidant function via glutathione and contributes to overall skin integrity and brightness.

Blend Uses: Ideal for anyone focused on aesthetic health like hair regrowth, improved skin quality, stronger nails, or as a foundational wellness protocol to replenish B-vitamin status. Also great for individuals under chronic stress, on restrictive diets, or experiencing hormonal shifts that affect hair and skin.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units. Frequency: Administered 2-5x per week.

NAD+ (20mg/mL): NAD+ is a coenzyme central to mitochondrial energy production and cellular repair. Levels decline with age and under metabolic stress, making supplementation especially impactful for energy, recovery, and body composition.

Blend Uses: Best used during cutting phases, fasted training, or as a general metabolic and energy support protocol.
