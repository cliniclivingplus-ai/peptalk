Course Overview: Injectable Amino & Vitamin Blends

This course breaks down different injectable amino acid and vitamin blends being used today for fat loss, performance, recovery, cognitive support, and overall health optimization.

5-Amino / NAD+ Blend

(NAD+ 100mg/mL / 5-Amino-1MQ 25mg/mL - 30mL)

This is a targeted two-compound blend focused on cellular energy production and fat metabolism at the mitochondrial level. NAD+ and 5-Amino-1MQ work complimentary to each other in a couple of ways - NAD+ fuels the mitochondria directly, while 5-Amino-1MQ inhibits an enzyme that would otherwise suppress fat cell activity and drain NAD+ reserves. Together they create a clean, stimulant-free environment for improved energy, body composition, and cellular function. This blend is popular for anti-aging protocols, metabolic optimization, and anyone looking to enhance fat loss without adding more stimulants or hormone-based compounds.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

NAD+ (100mg/mL): NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell in the body and is central to energy metabolism. It fuels the electron transport chain inside mitochondria, which is how your cells actually produce ATP (usable energy). NAD+ also activates sirtuins which are proteins involved in DNA repair, inflammation regulation, and longevity. Levels decline with age and metabolic stress, and restoring them through injection bypasses the digestive limitations of oral forms.

5-Amino-1MQ (25mg/mL): 5-Amino-1MQ is a selective NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase) inhibitor. NNMT is an enzyme that, when overactive, depletes NAD+ and keeps fat cells in a low-energy, storage-favoring state. By inhibiting NNMT, 5-Amino-1MQ allows NAD+ levels to rise naturally, activates dormant fat cells, and pushes them toward burning energy rather than storing it. Research has shown it can reduce fat cell size and support favorable changes in body composition without stimulating the central nervous system.

Blend Uses: Best used as part of a metabolic optimization, anti-aging, or body recomposition protocol. Particularly effective when stacked with lifestyle interventions like fasted training, caloric deficits, or time-restricted eating. Also used in longevity-focused protocols where restoring NAD+ levels and improving mitochondrial efficiency are the primary goals.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 20 - 50 units. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week. Some protocols run NAD+ daily or near-daily depending on goals.

5-Amino w/ NADH

This blend has ingredients that will help optimize the body at the cellular level. It can contribute to body recomposition, mitochondrial support, and longevity. It will contribute to energy output, metabolic flexibility, and can potentially turn off pathways that might slow metabolism with age.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

5-Amino-1MQ (50mg/mL): 5-Amino-1MQ inhibits NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase), which is an enzyme that can blunt metabolism and reduce NAD+ availability. By inhibiting NNMT, it can preserve NAD+, improve insulin sensitivity, and promote fat loss.

NADH (40mg/mL): This is a bioactive form of NAD+ that is critical for cellular energy, mitochondrial function, DNA repair, and longevity. NADH can increase ATP production, reduce brain fog and fatigue, and support healthy aging.

Blend Uses:

Pre-Workout Boost: This blend can be utilized as a pre-workout blend administered 30-45 minutes before a training session. It may be especially beneficial before fasted cardio to burn stubborn belly fat more efficiently.

General Fat Loss and Mitochondrial Health: This blend could me administered daily, every other day, or 5 on/2 off.

Common Dosage Ranges:

Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular.

Dosage (in units): 25 - 100 units.

Frequency: Administered on training days or as needed anywhere from 3-5x per week.

BCAA

This blend is focused on performance, endurance, and recovery. It combines a high dose of Arginine for blood flow with Carnosine for muscular endurance, plus a small dose of BCAAs for muscle support. Best used before training or during a high-output phase when you want better oxygen delivery, improved recovery between sets, and less muscular fatigue.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

Arginine (100mg/mL): Arginine boosts nitric oxide, which improves blood flow, vascularity, and nutrient delivery. This can help enhance endurance, reduce fatigue, and give a better “pump” during workouts. It also helps move waste products like ammonia out of muscles, keeping you going longer.

Carnosine (60mg/mL): Carnosine is a buffer against lactic acid. It helps delay muscle fatigue by reducing acid buildup during intense training. This can lead to more reps, better output, and improved recovery between sets. It’s also a powerful antioxidant at the muscular level.

Leucine (15mg/mL): Leucine flips on muscle protein synthesis via the mTOR pathway. Even in a fasted or depleted state, this helps trigger recovery and prevent breakdown. Dose is light, but supportive when stacked with other recovery tools.

Isoleucine (7.5mg/mL): Isoleucine helps shuttle glucose into muscle cells and supports endurance during long training sessions. Also assists in rebuilding muscle tissue post-workout.

Valine (7.5mg/mL): Valine acts as a fuel source for muscles during training and helps prevent muscle breakdown. It also supports focus and coordination, especially under fatigue.

Blend Uses:

Pre-Workout or Intra-Workout: Best used before or during training. Helps improve blood flow, muscular endurance, and recovery between sets. This blend is ideal for anyone doing high-volume lifting, circuit work, or endurance training where fatigue usually sets in fast.

Common Dosage Ranges:

Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular.

Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units.

Frequency: Administered 30-45 minutes before training 3-5x per week.

Threonine (335mg/mL): Threonine can support gut lining, mucin production, and tissue repair and is essential for joint, skin, and gut health. It can also contribute to muscle protein balance.

Lysine (35mg/mL): Lysine is essential for collagen synthesis, immune support, and calcium absorption and plays a key role in tissue regeneration. It can also pair well with threonine for gut and skin repair.

Post-Workout or Training Off-Days: This blend would be best utilized post-workout or on recovery days from workouts. It can help with gut repair, tissue support, muscle recovery, and can allow you to recover faster from difficult workouts.

Common Dosage Ranges:

Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular.

Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units.

Frequency: Administered post-workout or training off-days 3-5x per week.

Biotin (200mcg/mL): Biotin is very important for hair, skin, and nails and can support keratin structure and metabolism.

Riboflavin (200mcg/mL): Riboflavin is Vitamin B2 and helps to metabolize fats, proteins, and carbohydrates. It is a good antioxidant and is essential for red blood cell production and cellular respiration.

Glycine (40mg/mL): Glycine can help calm the nervous system, support collagen, and contribute to detoxification through glutathione production. It is great for skin, joint, and liver repair.

Proline (40mg/mL): Proline is an amino acid that is key for collagen and joint repair and can help support connective tissue, tendons, and elasticity.

Hydroxyproline (20mg/mL): Hydroxyproline is a collagen-specific amino acid that's only formed when your body is actively producing or breaking down collagen. This can directly support tissue healing as well as structural protein rebuilding.

Citrulline also increases nitric oxide with a longer-lasting effect and can work very effectively when paired with Arginine. This is great for pumps, vascularity, and endurance.

L-Glutamine (30mg/mL): Glutamine is a recovery compound that helps to repair muscle tissue and manage muscle soreness. Intense workouts and caloric deficits can burn through Glutamine quickly and this can help maintain that.

L-Taurine (45mg/mL): Taurine optimizes cell hydration and helps with endurance, limiting muscle cramps, and nervous system support. This will help keep muscles firing without the occurrence of cramps and mitigating failure due to fatigue.

L-Lysine (50mg/mL): Lysine is an amino that supports immune function, collagen production, and assists in converting fatty acids into energy and can work synergistically alongside of L-Carnitine in doing so.

L-Proline (45mg/mL): Proline can assist in supporting collagen, joint health, and tissue repair. It can be helpful in mitigating tendon and ligament stress due to heavy lifting or repetitive movements.

L-Carnitine (220mg/mL): L-Carnitine is a molecule that transports fat by moving it into the mitochondria to be burned for energy. It is fantastic for metabolic support, especially in fasted training or caloric deficits. Often times, people may experience increased endurance and stamina.

N-Acetylcysteine (NAC) (60mg/mL): NAC is a powerful antioxidant and glutathione precursor that can support liver detoxification, lung health, and can reduce oxidative stress from hard training. It can help keep inflammation in check and speed up recovery.

Blend Uses:

Pre-Workout Boost: This blend would be best utilized as a pre-workout blend administered 30-45 minutes before a training session. It covers a variety of things from blood flow and performance support, recovery and repair, and detoxification. This blend may allow you to push a little harder in the gym and recover quicker from workouts.

Method of administration: Intramuscular.

Dosage (in units): 25 - 100 units administered 30-45 minutes pre-workout.

HSN Blend

(Niacinamide 50mg/mL / Thiamine HCL 50mg/mL / Pantothenic Acid 25mg/mL / Choline 10mg/mL / Inositol 10mg/mL / Niacin 5mg/mL / Biotin 5mg/mL / Folic Acid 100mcg/mL / Riboflavin 100mcg/mL)

This is a comprehensive injectable B-vitamin and cofactor blend designed to support the building blocks of healthy hair, skin, and nails from the inside out. Each ingredient plays a specific role in cellular energy, keratin production, collagen synthesis, or follicle health. Unlike topical products that work on the surface, this blend delivers nutrients systemically, giving your body what it needs to actually build and repair tissue at the cellular level. It's also a solid general wellness protocol for energy, stress resilience, and metabolic support.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

Niacinamide (50mg/mL): Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) supports NAD+ production, which is central to cellular energy and DNA repair. For skin, it reduces inflammation, improves barrier function, and helps with hyperpigmentation. It also supports keratin production and is one of the most well-researched nutrients for skin health.

Thiamine HCL (50mg/mL): Thiamine (Vitamin B1) is essential for carbohydrate metabolism and nerve function. It plays a foundational role in cellular energy production and helps support the scalp's blood supply and follicle health. Deficiency is linked to hair thinning and poor skin quality.

Pantothenic Acid (25mg/mL): Vitamin B5 is directly involved in the synthesis of Coenzyme A, which is required for fatty acid metabolism and the production of steroid hormones. For hair specifically, it helps strengthen follicles, reduce shedding, and improve thickness. It also supports adrenal health and stress response.

Choline (10mg/mL): Choline supports cell membrane integrity and fat metabolism. Healthy, well-structured cell membranes are fundamental to skin elasticity and hydration. It also supports liver function, which plays a downstream role in hormone balance and skin clarity.

Inositol (10mg/mL): Inositol plays a role in cell signaling and insulin sensitivity. For skin and hair, it helps regulate sebum production and has been studied for its role in reducing hair loss associated with hormonal imbalances, particularly PCOS.

Niacin (5mg/mL): Niacin (a different form of B3 than niacinamide) dilates blood vessels and improves circulation to the scalp and skin. Improved circulation means better nutrient delivery to follicles and surface tissues, supporting both growth and overall skin tone.

Biotin (5mg/mL): Biotin is the most well-known nutrient for hair and nail health. It supports keratin infrastructure which is the structural protein that makes up hair and nails and is frequently linked to improved thickness, growth rate, and reduced brittleness when levels are restored.

Folic Acid (100mcg/mL): Folic acid (Vitamin B9) supports rapid cell division and DNA synthesis — processes that are especially active in hair follicles. It's important for hair growth cycling and helps prevent premature hair loss related to nutritional deficiency.

Riboflavin (100mcg/mL): Riboflavin (Vitamin B2) is a cofactor in the electron transport chain and plays a role in maintaining collagen and mucous membranes. It supports antioxidant function via glutathione and contributes to overall skin integrity and brightness.

Blend Uses: Ideal for anyone focused on aesthetic health like hair regrowth, improved skin quality, stronger nails, or as a foundational wellness protocol to replenish B-vitamin status. Also great for individuals under chronic stress, on restrictive diets, or experiencing hormonal shifts that affect hair and skin.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units. Frequency: Administered 2-5x per week.

L-Carnitine / NAD+ Blend

(L-Methionine / Inositol / Choline / L-Carnitine / B12 / B6 / NAD+ - 20mL)

This blend combines cellular energy production with fat metabolism and liver support. NAD+ works at the mitochondrial level to drive energy output and cellular repair, while L-Carnitine shuttles fatty acids into the mitochondria to be burned as fuel. Methionine, Inositol, and Choline handle liver function, methylation, and fat mobilization, and the B vitamins tie it all together for metabolic efficiency and recovery. This is a more advanced version of a standard lipotropic blend, with NAD+ elevating it into the cellular longevity and deep energy restoration category.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

L-Methionine (10mg/mL): Methionine is an essential amino acid that supports liver detox and methylation. It helps convert fat into usable energy and prevents fat buildup in the liver, which is especially relevant during cutting phases or periods of caloric restriction.

L-Inositol (50mg/mL): Inositol helps regulate blood sugar and improve insulin sensitivity, reducing cravings and supporting mood stability. It also plays a role in fat metabolism and can help blunt the hormonal stress response that often accompanies dieting.

L-Choline (50mg/mL): Choline is critical for fat transport out of the liver and supports acetylcholine production for mental focus and cognitive function. It works synergistically with carnitine and methionine to keep the liver clear and fat-burning pathways open.

L-Carnitine (250mg/mL): Carnitine is the workhorse of this blend. It physically transports long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria where they can be oxidized for energy. Especially effective during fasted training, cardio, or caloric deficits. Also supports endurance and reduces exercise-induced muscle soreness.

Vitamin B12 (500mcg/mL): B12 is essential for red blood cell production, DNA synthesis, and neurological function. It supports energy metabolism and is commonly depleted in individuals following restrictive diets. The injectable form ensures near-complete absorption compared to oral supplementation.

Vitamin B6 (25mg/mL): B6 supports amino acid metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, and hormone regulation. It plays a direct role in breaking down protein and fat for energy, and supports serotonin and dopamine production which is relevant for mood and motivation during a cut.

NAD+ (20mg/mL): NAD+ is a coenzyme central to mitochondrial energy production and cellular repair. It activates sirtuins (longevity proteins), supports DNA repair, and is a key driver of metabolic efficiency. Levels decline with age and under metabolic stress, making supplementation especially impactful for energy, recovery, and body composition.

Blend Uses: Best used during cutting phases, fasted training, or as a general metabolic and energy support protocol. The NAD+ component adds a longevity and cellular health angle that makes this appropriate beyond just fat loss and it's solid for anyone wanting to optimize energy output, improve recovery, and support liver function simultaneously.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week.

NAD+ works at the mitochondrial level to drive energy output and cellular repair, while L-Carnitine shuttles fatty acids into the mitochondria to be burned as fuel. Methionine, Inositol, and Choline handle liver function, methylation, and fat mobilization, and the B vitamins tie it all together for metabolic efficiency and recovery. This is a more advanced version of a standard lipotropic blend, with NAD+ elevating it into the cellular longevity and deep energy restoration category.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

L-Methionine (10mg/mL): Methionine is an essential amino acid that supports liver detox and methylation. It helps convert fat into usable energy and prevents fat buildup in the liver, which is especially relevant during cutting phases or periods of caloric restriction.

L-Inositol (50mg/mL): Inositol helps regulate blood sugar and improve insulin sensitivity, reducing cravings and supporting mood stability. It also plays a role in fat metabolism and can help blunt the hormonal stress response that often accompanies dieting.

L-Choline (50mg/mL): Choline is critical for fat transport out of the liver and supports acetylcholine production for mental focus and cognitive function. It works synergistically with carnitine and methionine to keep the liver clear and fat-burning pathways open.

L-Carnitine (250mg/mL): Carnitine is the workhorse of this blend. It physically transports long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria where they can be oxidized for energy. Especially effective during fasted training, cardio, or caloric deficits. Also supports endurance and reduces exercise-induced muscle soreness.

Vitamin B6 (25mg/mL): B6 supports amino acid metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, and hormone regulation. It plays a direct role in breaking down protein and fat for energy, and supports serotonin and dopamine production which is relevant for mood and motivation during a cut.

NAD+ (20mg/mL): NAD+ is a coenzyme central to mitochondrial energy production and cellular repair. It activates sirtuins (longevity proteins), supports DNA repair, and is a key driver of metabolic efficiency. Levels decline with age and under metabolic stress, making supplementation especially impactful for energy, recovery, and body composition.

Blend Uses: Best used during cutting phases, fasted training, or as a general metabolic and energy support protocol. The NAD+ component adds a longevity and cellular health angle that makes this appropriate beyond just fat loss and it's solid for anyone wanting to optimize energy output, improve recovery, and support liver function simultaneously.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week.

Pumptira

(L-Carnitine 200mg/mL / Arginine 100mg/mL / Ornithine 100mg/mL / Citrulline 100mg/mL / Methionine 10mg/mL / Choline 50mg/mL)

This blend is built for performance. It combines three of the most well-studied nitric oxide and ammonia-clearing compounds which are Arginine, Ornithine, and Citrulline, with Carnitine for fat-fueled energy, and Methionine and Choline for liver support and focus. The result is a clean, stimulant-free pre-training blend that enhances blood flow, delays fatigue, and keeps your metabolism running efficiently during the session. Great for anyone who trains hard and wants more out of their workout without adding stimulants.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

L-Carnitine (200mg/mL): Carnitine shuttles long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria to be burned as fuel. It supports energy production during training, reduces exercise-induced muscle damage, and can enhance endurance especially during fasted sessions or caloric deficits.

Arginine (100mg/mL): Arginine is a direct precursor to nitric oxide, a molecule that relaxes and widens blood vessels. Improved circulation means better oxygen and nutrient delivery to working muscles, better pumps, and improved performance output during training.

Ornithine (100mg/mL): Ornithine plays a key role in the urea cycle, helping the body clear ammonia which is a byproduct of intense exercise that contributes directly to fatigue. Clearing it faster means you can push harder and longer before hitting the wall.

Citrulline (100mg/mL): Citrulline converts to Arginine in the kidneys, effectively sustaining nitric oxide production longer than Arginine alone. It's one of the most studied ergogenic compounds for endurance, pumps, and reducing muscle soreness after training.

Methionine (10mg/mL): Methionine supports methylation and liver detox, helping your body process and eliminate metabolic waste efficiently. During hard training, this keeps the metabolic environment cleaner and supports overall recovery.

Choline (50mg/mL): Choline supports acetylcholine production which is the neurotransmitter responsible for muscle contraction signaling and mental focus. It also supports fat metabolism and liver function, keeping energy pathways clear during training.

Blend Uses: Best used pre-training for enhanced pumps, blood flow, and endurance. Also solid as a general performance and recovery support blend on non-training days. Works well stacked with peptide protocols for anyone focused on body recomposition.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week, ideally 30-60 minutes before training.

Zeus

This amino blend has that can be used to boost recovery and energy. Ingredients within this blend will help with fatigue, clearing waste, rebuilding tissue, and boosting energy through the mitochondria.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

L-Arginine (110mg/mL): Arginine is a nitric oxide booster which helps to open blood vessels and improve circulation and nutrient delivery. This will lead to better pumps (muscle fullness feeling), better oxygenation, and potentially better endurance in the gym. Using this as an injectable can give a higher absorption rate and maximize the benefits of the compound.

L-Glycine (40mg/mL): Glycine is an amino acid that can help with sleep, recovery, collagen, and glutathione production. It can also act as a neurotransmitter that calms the Central Nervous System (CNS) post-exertion.

L-Taurine (75mg/mL): Taurine optimizes cell hydration and helps with endurance, limiting muscle cramps, and nervous system support. This will help keep muscles firing without the occurrence of cramps and mitigating failure due to fatigue.

NADH (40mg/mL): NADH is an active form of NAD+ which is used by your mitochondria to product ATP (energy). It is used essentially as "cellular fuel" to increase endurance, cellular repair, cognitive clarity, and metabolism.

L-Glutamine (40mg/mL): Glutamine is a recovery compound that helps to repair muscle tissue and manage muscle soreness. Intense workouts and caloric deficits can burn through Glutamine quickly and this can help maintain that.

L-Lysine (75mg/mL): Lysine is an amino that supports immune function, collagen production, and assists in converting fatty acids into energy and can work synergistically alongside of L-Carnitine in doing so.

L-Ornithine (85mg/mL): Ornithine can help with ammonia clearance. Intense training can cause ammonia to build up and lead to fatigue. Ornithine can help the liver and kidneys clear this ammonia and reduce fatigue. It can work alongside of Arginine to boost Nitric Oxide and recovery.

BCAAs (2:1:1 ratio) (20mg/mL): Branched-chain aminos (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine) can help prevent muscle breakdown and can support intra/post-workout recovery.

Post-Workout Support: This blend could be administered post-workout to lead to better recovery and energy. It would be best utilized after difficult training days, post cardio, or on off-days to keep the body fueled and recover better.

Common Dosage Ranges:

Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular.

Dosage (in units): 50 - 100 units administered 30-45 minutes post-workout.

Frequency: Administered on training days or as needed anywhere from 3-5x per week.

SHREDTIRA

(L-Carnitine 400mg/mL / MIC Blend 100mg/mL / ATP 50mg/mL / Albuterol 2mg/mL)

SHREDTIRA is not a beginner blend. It combines one of the highest-concentration injectable Carnitine doses available with the lipotropic MIC trio, ATP for direct cellular energy, and Albuterol which a beta-2 agonist with well-documented thermogenic and lipolytic properties. This is a purpose-built fat loss protocol designed for individuals who are already managing their diet and training, and want a pharmacological edge in mobilizing and burning stored fat. It's aggressive, targeted, and effective when used appropriately.

Ingredient List and Purpose:

L-Carnitine (400mg/mL): At this concentration, Carnitine provides maximum fatty acid transport into the mitochondria. During caloric deficits or fasted training, this means more fat is pulled from storage and burned for fuel rather than muscle glycogen. Also supports endurance, reduces soreness, and improves overall recovery.

MIC Blend - Methionine / Inositol / Choline (100mg/mL combined): The classic lipotropic trio working together. Methionine handles methylation and liver detox, Inositol regulates insulin sensitivity and fat signaling, and Choline clears fat from the liver and keeps cognitive function sharp. Together they create a clean metabolic environment that supports the fat mobilization the rest of this blend is driving.

ATP - Adenosine Triphosphate (50mg/mL): ATP is the direct energy currency of every cell in the body. Injectable ATP bypasses the need for metabolic conversion and delivers immediate energy substrate to tissues. It supports muscular endurance, can improve training output, and may enhance the rate at which fatty acids are burned during exercise by fueling the cellular machinery that drives oxidation.

Albuterol (2mg/mL): Albuterol is a beta-2 adrenergic agonist most commonly known as a bronchodilator, but it has well-established fat loss and muscle-sparing properties. By stimulating beta-2 receptors, it increases thermogenesis (heat production), boosts lipolysis (fat breakdown from cells), and can help preserve lean mass during a cut. It acts similarly to Clenbuterol but with a shorter half-life and a generally more manageable side effect profile.

Blend Uses: Designed for accelerated fat loss phases, competition prep, or stubborn body fat protocols. Best used alongside a structured diet and training program — this blend amplifies what's already working, it doesn't replace the fundamentals. Due to the Albuterol component, cardiovascular tolerance should be assessed before use and dosing started conservatively.

Common Dosage Ranges: Method of administration: Subcutaneous or intramuscular. Dosage (in units): 25 units MAX to start and titrate based on tolerance. Frequency: Administered 3-5x per week. Albuterol sensitivity varies - assess tolerance at lower doses before increasing frequency.
