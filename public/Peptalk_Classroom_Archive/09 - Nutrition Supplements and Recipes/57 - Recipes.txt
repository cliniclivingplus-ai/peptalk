Egg White Omelette (Base Recipe with Variations)

Ingredients (1 serving):

6 egg whites

Optional: 1 whole egg

Optional mix-ins: 1/4 cup sautéed onions and bell peppers, OR onions and mushrooms

Optional: 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella (part-skim)

Instructions:
Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add your preferred sautéed vegetables. Pour in egg whites (and whole egg if using). Cook until set, folding gently. Top with mozzarella if using and let melt before serving.

Macros (base with 6 egg whites):
Calories: ~102
Protein: ~21g
Carbs: ~1g
Fat: ~0g
(Add ~70 cal / 6g fat / 6g protein for whole egg, and ~80 cal / 6g fat / 7g protein for cheese)

Protein Oat & Berry Yogurt Bowl
Quick high-protein breakfast with balanced carbs and antioxidants.

Ingredients (1 serving):

170g Oikos Pro Vanilla Greek Yogurt (2% or Triple Zero)

40g raw old-fashioned oats

110g frozen blueberries

Instructions:

In a bowl, combine yogurt and oats. Stir until evenly mixed.

Add frozen blueberries directly or thaw slightly for easier mixing.

Optional: Let sit for 5–10 minutes to soften oats or refrigerate overnight for a cold “overnight oats” style texture.

Enjoy cold!

Estimated Macros (1 serving):
Calories: ~360
Protein: ~28g
Carbs: ~42g
Fiber: ~6g
Net Carbs: ~36g
Fat: ~5g

Macros may vary slightly depending on the exact Oikos variety used (Triple Zero vs Pro vs regular). Triple Zero has no added sugar and keeps fat low.

Egg White Veggie Bake with Cottage Cheese

(Easy breakfast prep)

Ingredients:

2 cups egg whites

1/2 cup low-fat cottage cheese

1/2 cup shredded zucchini

1/2 cup chopped peppers

1/4 cup diced onion

Seasonings: salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian herbs

Instructions:
Mix everything in a bowl. Pour into a greased 8x8 baking dish. Bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes until firm. Slice into squares.

Macros (1 of 4):
~110 cal / 16g protein / 4g carbs / 3g fat

Sweet Potato Hash with Turkey & Eggs

(Savory meal-prep breakfast)

Ingredients:

1 lb lean ground turkey

2 cups diced sweet potatoes

1/2 cup diced onion

1/2 cup chopped spinach

4 eggs (or 1 egg + 4 egg whites for leaner version)

Olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika

Instructions:
Roast sweet potatoes at 400°F for 20 minutes. Brown turkey and onions in a skillet. Add spinach and cooked potatoes. Crack eggs over the hash, cover, and cook until yolks set.

Macros (1 of 4 servings):
~350 cal / 28g protein / 20g carbs / 16g fat

Cottage Cheese Banana Oat Protein Pancake Bites 🥞

Text saved

🛒 Ingredients:

1 ripe banana, mashed

1/2 cup cottage cheese

1/2 cup rolled oats

2 large eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

1/2 teaspoon baking powder

1/4 teaspoon salt

1/4 cup mini chocolate chips (optional)

Cooking spray or butter (for greasing the muffin tin)

👩‍🍳 Directions:

Preheat the Oven:

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a mini muffin tin with cooking spray or butter.

Blend the Ingredients:

In a blender, combine the mashed banana, cottage cheese, oats, eggs, vanilla extract, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt. Blend until smooth.

Fold in Chocolate Chips (Optional):

If using, stir in the mini chocolate chips by hand.

Fill the Muffin Tin:

Pour the batter into the greased mini muffin tin, filling each cup about 3/4 full.

Bake:

Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the pancake bites are puffed up and golden around the edges.

Cool and Serve:

Let the pancake bites cool in the pan for 5 minutes before removing them. Serve warm with maple syrup, fresh fruit, or enjoy them on their own!

🍽 Serves: 12-14 pancake bites

⏲️ Prep Time: 5 minutes

⏲️ Cook Time: 12-15 minutes

⏲️ Total Time: 20 minutes

🔥 Calories: 60 calories per bite
