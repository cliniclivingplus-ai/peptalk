Testing First

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/50863d37?md=2e766eeab7224d8695b437281d49f56a

Text saved

If you want to get results with supplementation, then the most important thing you can do before buying any is get blood work done. The reason for this is pretty simple. Deficiency and sufficiency are going to look very different when it comes to supplementation. Taking vitamin D when you are already at optimal levels is probably going to do very little for you but taking it when you are severely deficient can literally change your life. It's still the same compound but you have significantly different outcomes depending on where your baseline is. 

Most people don't test any of this stuff and they just guess that they're deficient in something based on symptoms, which is really not reliable especially when there's a lot of similarity between different causal factors. So by assuming that most people are deficient in a certain thing and taking it because of that isn't necessarily wrong as a place to start but it's not really a strategized approach to it. In this course you will see that there is a testing guide that covers exactly what to test, what those results might mean, and then the ranges you might be targeting instead of the broad reference ranges that doctors might say are normal.



What Supplements Can and Can't Do

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/50863d37?md=1174d30e3bf944f084589967da90e407

Text saved

Supplements can not override a poor diet, chronic sleep deprivation, lack of exercise, or high stress. What they can do is fill genuine gaps in nutrition that the diet doesn't cover. Whether it's because some of the modern food is less nutrient-dense than it used to be, because you have some type of genetic variant that affects how you process certain nutrients (the MTHFR section covers this in depth), or because your lifestyle creates demands that food alone doesn't meet. 

Some of these compounds do have some legitimate longevity, performance, and neuroprotective effects that go beyond just correcting a deficiency but again these things are still going to want to have a foundation that is solid.
