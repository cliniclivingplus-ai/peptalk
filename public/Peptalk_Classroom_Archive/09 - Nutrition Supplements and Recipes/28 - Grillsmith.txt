Steakzilla's Rib Recipe

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/0938de07?md=f1dcdf1e3598453a9daa7f4cb9a5e7bf

Text saved

Here is my method- 275F until color is good and bark is set. This could be an hour or two. Depending on humidity. Wrap them in double foil, meat side up, with whatever mix you want to use. (I.e. butter, apple juice, some sauce, sugar in the raw) Then go to temp. For me in Kansas, it is 212F for fall off the bone, 208F for bite thru. Rest them wrapped in a cooler for at least an hour. Take the rack out of the foil, sauce, and put back on smoker for 5-10 minutes. Done. Oh and heat sauce up before applying to ribs. Quick and easy sauce mix everyone loves- Head Country Apple Habanero, Blues Hog Original. 2-1 ratio. You go to color and if the bark is set by touching it and nothing sticks to your finger. With humidity being crazy lately, my color and bark are set in an hour.

Smoke Pork Tenderlions

From Jennifer: 

lovvve to smoke meat! My favorite and go to (besides ribs) is to smoke pork tenderloins, (4 at a time fit nicely in my little masterbuilt)and freeze them for later. It’s super quick, easy, great texture moist, high protein, low calorie and full of flavor!

I use a-1 as a binder, rub jerk pork seasoning (Penzeys is the way)

225-250 depending on how fast you want to cook it

Smoke to Internal temp 140-145 (about an hour or so)

Remove

Add bbq sauce if you like it saucey

wrap to rest- about 20 min or longer

Leftovers make a bomb ass pho-like noodle soup

The Great Hambino’s Pulled Pork

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/0938de07?md=9f6d448386a542ffb30cf905dc47ee98

Text saved

Preheat smoker to 225

Season with your favorite seasoning. I prefer Kinders seasonings but anything works! Pork pairs well with BBQ, sweet, or savory flavors.

I prefer smoking with Apple pellets but any pellet will do, its just personal preference

Place the shoulder on the smoker at 225 til it hits 160-170 internal. 

    

***Not necessary but you can spritz every hour with apple juice. It keeps the top layer moist and  will help build the bark and give it good flavor. You can also add a light coat of seasoning when you do this.***

Once it hits 165, take it off and wrap it in butcher paper. If you don’t have butcher paper, tinfoil is fine but it will just soften the bark. I do 2 layers and I wrap it tight.

After you wrap it, put it back on the smoker at 225 til it hits 202 internal 

Once it hits 202 take it off and let it rest for at least 30 minutes to an hour then unwrap it and shred away! 

***If it finishes earlier than you were expecting DON’T WORRY! You can pull it off the smoker, keeping it wrapped in the butcher paper, wrap it in a large towel and place it in a cooler. It will stay hot for a couple hours! The longer it sits the better it gets!

Expect cook times to be long – an average of 1.5 hours per pound.
