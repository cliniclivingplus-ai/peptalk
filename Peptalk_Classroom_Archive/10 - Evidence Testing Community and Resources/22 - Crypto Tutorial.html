Using CashApp to Pay Bitcoin

The video above is going to tell you how to pay with Bitcoin. If you are checking out from a website like CosmicNootropic, they will put up a picture of a QR code and wallet. If you click that, it will take you to CashApp to pay. Just make sure you have enough BTC in the wallet to cover any fees as well. I usually have about $15 extra in BTC in the wallet for this scenario.
