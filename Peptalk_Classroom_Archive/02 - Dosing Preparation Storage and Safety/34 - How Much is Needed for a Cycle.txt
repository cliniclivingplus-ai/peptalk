The Basics

https://www.skool.com/hacksmithspeptalk/classroom/fc2754d5?md=45c51755d0d844efa6aa9096ed529e2e

Text saved

Before you try to figure out how much to buy, you have to understand the dosing schedule and frequency. Some peptides are taken daily, some might be 5 days a week, and some are taken once a week. This is important to understand, because it changes how much you will use each week as well as over the course of the cycle. 

Here's the information you need to figure out: 

How much do you need for each dose? 

How many times per week are you taking that dose? 

How many weeks are you running the cycle? 

Will you increase the dosage over the course of the cycle? 

How many vials will that add up to based on the total amount of mg in the vial? 

Peptides are typically labeled by milligrams (mg) vs mL, which can throw some people off. You are getting a certain number of mg (typically in a 3mL vial) and then you are adding the liquid. What is really important is how much of the compound is actually in the vial. 

Let's say that you're dosing BPC-157 at 250mcg daily. A 5mg vial would be 5000mcg. If you take it daily, then that vial will last you 20 days. If you are doing it 5 days per week, that will last you 4 weeks. This is the type of logic that you have to apply to this and I will try to teach you in this course.



Daily Dosing Example

Text saved

This section is going to break down BPC-157 used daily as an example for how to calculate based off of the length of time you want to use it. 

Assumptions: 

250mcg per day dosage

Taken daily (7 days per week)

6 week length of cycle

Step 1: Figure out weekly dose

250mcg per day x 7 days = 1,750mcg per week

Step 2: Multiply by the number of weeks

1,750mcg/week x 6 weeks = 10,500mcg total

10,500mcg is equal to 10.5mg

Step 3: Determine the vial size you're going to get

Let's assume BPC-157 vials are 5mg each

You'll need at least 3 vials of 5mg to cover that 10.5mg, or adjust your cycle length a couple of days short to give an even 10mg amount. 

If you got 3x 5mg vials, you would have 15 total mg, which is enough to cover the 10.5 mg. Or you could extend the cycle to be longer to use the entire 15mg.



5x Weekly Dosing Example

Text saved

This next example will cover a peptide that is being used 5 days per week, like CJC-1295/Ipamorelin since those are typically 5 days a week as opposed to 7. 

Assumptions: 

300mcg per dose (of each)

Dosed 5 days per week

Cycle length is 8 weeks

Step 1: Determine weekly dose

300mcg x 5 days = 1,500mcg per week

Step 2: Determine total cycle dose

1,500mcg x 8 weeks = 12,000mcg total

12,000mcg is 12mg for the entire 8 week cycle

Step 3: How many vials are needed? 

If the vials are 5mg each, you will need 3 vials to cover the 12mg which would give you 15mg total. You could extend the length of the cycle to use the entirety of this, or you will have some left over unused.



Once Per Week Dosing Example

For this example, we are going to use something typically dosed weekly by using a GLP. Since they typically aren't ran in cycles, we are going to cover how much you will need for a 2 month period as an example. 

Assumptions: 

Tirzepatide dosed at 7.5mg per week

Dosed 1x weekly

Need enough for 2 months

Step 1: Determine total amount needed: 

7.5mg x 8 weeks is 60mg total. Therefore, you will need 60mg total to last you for 2 months. 

Step 2: Determine how many vials are needed: 

For the purposes of calculating, we'll use a 20mg vial of Tirzepatide. 

You will need at least 3x 20mg vials to cover those 2 months. Or, you could get 1x 60mg vial.



Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

I've given examples for how to calculate the total amount based on frequency, but want to make this simple so it can be repeated across different compounds and don't have to guess. 

Step 1: Determine the frequency: 

Daily (7x weekly)

5x weekly

Once weekly

Once you figure this out, plug in your dose per injection in micrograms (mcg) or milligrams (mg). 

Step 2: Multiply it out: 

Daily: 

Dose x 7 = weekly total

Weekly total x number of weeks = total amount needed

5x Weekly: 

Dose x 5 = weekly total

Weekly total x number of weeks = total amount needed

Weekly: 

Dose x number of weeks = total amount needed

Convert micrograms to milligrams if needed: 

1,000mcg = 1mg

Step 3: Figure out how many vials are needed: 

Once you know your total mg needed, divide by the vial size. 

If you need 12mg total and you're buying 5mg vials: 

12 / 5 = 2.4, so round up to 3 vials. Always round up as you can't use half of a vial you don't buy.
