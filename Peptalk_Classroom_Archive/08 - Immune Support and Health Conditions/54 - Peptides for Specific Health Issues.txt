This guide is a reference guide for different health conditions and which peptides may help to support them. This is not medical advice and you should research the what, why, and how of each of these and how they could be supportive of each of these issues. 

The primary peptides are the ones that are either most direct in fixing the issue, fix the root of the issue, or are likely to be most effective overall. The supportive peptides are going to support symptoms or other things associated with these issues, or it may just be a lesser preferred option for the specific issue.
